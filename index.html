About me

I have always been drawn to problems that require structure and clear thinking. Growing up with a natural inclination toward mathematics, it felt like a natural path to pursue it seriously at Kennesaw State University, where I am studying Mathematics with a minor in Applied Statistics and Data Analysis.

What pulled me deeper into data analysis was realizing how much is hidden in plain sight. Behind every dataset is a story waiting to be told, a pattern worth uncovering, or a process that could work better. Learning to find those things through various programming tools and diverse datasets became something I genuinely enjoyed rather than just studied.

I want to work on problems where the analysis actually changes something, whether that means helping a team make a better decision, identifying where something is breaking down, or presenting findings in a way that non-technical stakeholders can understand and act on. Data is most impactful when it is delivered clearly to the people who need it, and that is the standard I hold my work to.
